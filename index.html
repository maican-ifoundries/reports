00 — Executive Summary

Fewer people are finding e2i in Search this month — but far more of them are converting.

The story across all three data sources is consistent: technical health is strong, on-site conversion behaviour has never been better, and the growth constraint right now sits at the top of the funnel — visibility and click-through in Search.

What's working

Organic key events jumped from 3,453 to 10,260 month‑on‑month — a 197% lift — and the user key‑event rate climbed from 9.5% to 16.25%. Year‑on‑year the gap is even starker: key events are up 2,314%, off a low base but with a rate that's genuinely transformed, from 2.37% to 16.25%. Average Search position also improved dramatically year‑on‑year, from 27.9 to 10.1.

The site itself is in excellent technical shape: an Ahrefs Health Score of 97, with only 117 errors against 2,965 crawled URLs.

Where to focus

  • ▼ Search Console clicks fell 5.5% MoM (14.5K → 13.7K) and 27.1% YoY (18.8K → 13.7K), even as impressions rose 9.2% MoM.
  • ▼ Click‑through rate slipped from 4.7% to 4.1% MoM and from 4.8% to 4.1% YoY — impressions aren't converting to clicks as efficiently.
  • ! Average position worsened MoM, from 7.8 to 10.1, even though it's dramatically better than a year ago.
  • ! 264 pages link internally to redirects, 255 pages are flagged slow, and 226 have overly long titles — all suppressing CTR and crawl efficiency.

04 — Recommendations

Three levers, prioritised by what the data shows

Grouped by the layer of the funnel each one addresses — technical health, on‑page relevance, and content & authority.

Technical

Clear the crawl & link debt

  • Fix or redirect the 46 pages linking to broken pages, plus 19 404s and 19 other 4XX responses.
  • Repoint the 264 pages that link to redirects, straight to their final URL.
  • Compress the 22 oversized images flagged in the crawl.
  • Add internal links into the 9 orphan pages so they can be crawled and ranked.
  • Correct the 1 HTTPS→HTTP internal link and the 1 3XX redirect inside the sitemap.
Basis: Ahrefs Top Issues — errors & warnings, Jul 2026
On‑page & CTR

Win back the click‑through rate

  • Rewrite the 226 title tags flagged as too long, prioritising the highest‑impression URLs first.
  • Refresh meta descriptions on pages where impressions are rising but clicks aren't following.
  • Investigate the 255 slow pages — Core Web Vitals affect both rankings and perceived relevance.
  • Audit what changed on pages that slipped from position 7.8 to 10.1 month‑on‑month.
Basis: GSC CTR ↓4.7%→4.1% MoM, position ↓7.8→10.1 MoM
Content & Authority

Rebuild reach without losing quality

  • Map the query gap behind the 15% YoY drop in impressions and brief new content against it.
  • Identify the pages driving the 197% MoM jump in key events and replicate their structure across other landing pages.
  • Build internal links from high‑traffic pages into conversion‑ready pages to spread the lift.
  • Pursue relevant backlinks to rebuild topical authority alongside the content push.
Basis: GSC impressions ↓15.2% YoY, GA4 key‑event rate ↑585.7% YoY
